MKTG 304 | Week 10 tutorial

Vibe coding for digital marketers

Our last tutorial of the semester, and a chance to build something you can actually use.

This week's tutorial introduces vibe coding: using natural language prompts to build working digital tools rather than written content. Until now you have mostly used AI to research, draft and summarise. This week you will use it to produce artefacts you can use, share and add to your portfolio.

Both activities are built around your own work for Music Planet, so what you make is directly useful for A3 and your A4 defence.

What you will build

Activity 1 · Claude or ChatGPT

A budget and KPI planner for your A3 campaign

You will build an interactive tool that takes your campaign goal, the objective that supports it, the tactic you have chosen and its share of the $10,000 budget. It returns the KPIs that matter, the benchmark assumptions behind them, projected outcomes over 30 days and whether the tactic can plausibly reach your objective. You will then check the numbers: which figures can you verify, and which are assumptions you need to label as such?

Activity 2 · Lovable

A landing page for your A3 campaign

You will use Lovable to build a live, shareable landing page for the campaign your team is proposing to Music Planet, complete with a campaign promo code or tracked call to action. Before anything is shared, you will swap pages with another team and test each other's work for invented claims, broken buttons and mobile problems.

How we will work

Both builds use the CRAFT framework and the Meta-CRAFT prompt from Week 2. Your Client Intelligence Brief becomes the Context for every prompt you write. The quality of your prompt decides the quality of what you build, and that connection is the central lesson of the session.

The tutorial closes with another round of IOA practice, building on last week's session, to help you prepare for your A4 defence.

Before you come

Set up a free account on Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT, and a free account on Lovable (lovable.dev). Free Lovable accounts have a limited number of daily credits, so you will draft your prompt before you open it.

Bring your Client Intelligence Brief from Week 2 and a short summary of your team's A3 campaign concept.
